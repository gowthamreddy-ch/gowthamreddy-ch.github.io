1. Feedback: choose Explore saved example. It is hand-authored, with no AI call. Expand a source, edit a summary, and mark each theme kept or rejected. Export requires all themes to be reviewed.
2. Recovery: inspect the simulated device and its drift. Preview a proposed action and inspect the diff. Apply only when you intend to change the simulated state and approve that exact proposal. No physical hardware is controlled.

4. Read the dated results and limitations. Synthetic label agreement is not customer validation. Recorded live results are separate from saved examples.

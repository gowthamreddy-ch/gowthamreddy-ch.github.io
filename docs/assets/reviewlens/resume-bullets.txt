# Evidence-based resume bullets

- Built ReviewLens, a React/TypeScript code-review lab with a Node API, persistent
  SQLite review queue, background analysis, finding feedback and approval-gated
  draft export for synthetic TypeScript/JavaScript pull requests.
- Implemented AST-based checks and two structured-output AI review modes with
  changed-line/evidence validation, idempotent submissions, repository-scoped
  storage, bounded retries and interruption recovery; verified boundaries with
  automated tests and desktop/mobile browser checks.
- Compared static, diff-only and context-assisted reviewers on ten authored
  development cases; preserved raw outputs and documented false positives,
  unsupported reasoning, latency and token-cost estimates under a predefined rubric.

Do not claim production adoption, GitHub App deployment, independent human
evaluation, held-out performance, vulnerability prevention, or time savings.
Keep exact metrics with their synthetic-development denominator and audit method
in the case study rather than implying general effectiveness in a short bullet.

02 / Engineering

Proposed scope & evaluation

Problem and scope

An assistant may produce a plausible recommendation even when important evidence is missing. The proposed study would begin with a narrowly defined incident-triage task.

Intended contribution

Plan the study, prepare evaluation cases, document a baseline and rubric, and analyze behavior. These are intended contributions to future work.

Proposed architecture and data flow

A proposed evaluation workflow would connect versioned cases and configurations with captured outputs, human scoring, and a reproducible report.

Proposed technology choices and tradeoffs

Begin with a single task and explicit criteria for supported recommendations. Choose data, tools, and model configurations after the research question and access constraints are settled.

Planned evaluation

The existing triage rebuild has synthetic diagnosis and reference-validation results. This proposed controlled study of incomplete and conflicting evidence has not been completed; it will require its own protocol, scoring and independent review.

Limitations and next steps

This is an initial research plan. Dataset access, labeling consistency, case coverage, and supervisor feedback will shape the final scope.
